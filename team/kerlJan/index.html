About BHIS

Your community's health is our priority

BHIS is a role-based web application built specifically for barangay health centers. It replaces manual paper records with a streamlined digital system that tracks prenatal care, child immunization, chronic disease management, and generates FHSIS reports automatically.

Designed for three distinct user roles — Admin, Midwife, and BHW — each with their own dedicated dashboard and feature set. The system supports offline data entry for field health workers and ensures complete data privacy through comprehensive audit logging.

System Roles

Three roles, one system

Each role has a dedicated dashboard with features tailored to their responsibilities. Role-based access ensures data security and proper separation of duties.

👤
System Administrator
Manages user accounts, monitors activity through audit logs, and configures system settings.
  • Create, edit & deactivate users
  • View full audit trail
  • Export logs to PDF / CSV
  • Configure barangay settings
  • Reset user passwords
🏥
Licensed Barangay Midwife
Tracks health programs and generates official FHSIS & MHO reports for submission.
  • Prenatal care & visit logging
  • Child immunization tracking
  • Senior / chronic disease records
  • Overdue & high-risk alerts
  • Monthly report generation
🏘️
Barangay Health Worker
Monitors prenatal and child immunization records in the field.
  • View prenatal records
  • Search children profiles
  • Field health monitoring
  • Offline data access
  • Pending sync tracking
