About BHW Dashboard

Empowering the first line of care

Barangay Health Workers (BHWs) are the crucial link between the health center and the community. This dashboard provides the tools needed to collect accurate, real-time data from every household.

By digitizing household profiling and nutrition tracking, BHWs can quickly identify at-risk families and ensure that health interventions are delivered precisely where they are needed most.

Health Programs

Four integrated community modules

Designed for rapid data entry and comprehensive community tracking, these modules form the foundation of barangay health records.

🏠
Household Profiling
Comprehensive tracking of families and living conditions.
  • Register household head & members
  • Log water & sanitation facilities
  • Record socioeconomic indicators
  • Search households by Purok/Zone
👨‍👩‍👧
Family Planning
Monitor contraceptive use and resupply schedules.
  • Register WRA (Women of Repro. Age)
  • Log current contraceptive method
  • Track resupply/check-up dates
  • Identify drop-outs for follow-up
🍎
Nutrition Assessment
Monitor child growth and combat malnutrition.
  • Register children under 5
  • Log periodic weight and height
  • Track nutritional status (OPT Plus)
  • Manage supplementary feeding
📝
Community Surveys
Rapid data collection for targeted health initiatives.
  • Log environmental sanitation checks
  • Track disease outbreak vectors (e.g., Dengue)
  • Record maternal/child health census
  • Generate community summaries
