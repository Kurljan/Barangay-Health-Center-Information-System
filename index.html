About BHIS

Your community's health is our priority

Kibalabag BHIS is a role-based web application built specifically for the Kibalabag barangay health center. It replaces manual paper records with a streamlined digital system that tracks prenatal care, child immunization, and generates FHSIS reports automatically.

Designed for three distinct user roles — Admin, Midwife, and BHW — each with their own dedicated dashboard and feature set. The system ensures complete data privacy through comprehensive audit logging.

System Roles

Three roles, one system

Each role has a dedicated dashboard with features tailored to their responsibilities. Role-based access ensures data security and proper separation of duties.

System Administrator
Manages user accounts, monitors activity through audit logs, and configures system settings.
  • Create, edit & deactivate users
  • View full audit trail
  • Export logs to PDF / CSV
  • Configure barangay settings
  • Reset user passwords
Licensed Barangay Midwife
Tracks health programs and generates official FHSIS & MHO reports for submission. (Head Midwife: Shelajoy Libuhan)
  • Prenatal care & visit logging
  • Child immunization tracking
  • Overdue & high-risk alerts
  • Monthly report generation
Barangay Health Worker
Assists with community health tracking, including prenatal and immunization records.
  • Access to prenatal records
  • Search child immunization records
  • Health condition tagging
